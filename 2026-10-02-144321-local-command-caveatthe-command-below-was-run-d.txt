  The research answer: no open-weight model that goes straight from voice to
  voice beats a well-built pipeline on weak hardware.
  - The only one that runs on an 8 GB machine is LFM2.5-Audio-1.5B. It speaks
    only English, can't listen while it talks, and its answers are noticeably
    weaker.
  - The ones that can talk and listen at the same time (Moshi, PersonaPlex,
    MiniCPM-o) need 16 GB or more.
  - Kyutai, who made Moshi, built their own product as a pipeline for the same
    reasons.

  What to change in your setup:
  - Replace Supertonic. It was archived on 2026-09-09, and it renders a whole
    sentence before you hear anything (about 600 ms). Kyutai's Pocket TTS starts
    playing within about 70 ms because it streams.
  - Speech-to-text: Moonshine v2 streaming. It transcribes while you talk, so
    the text is ready about 75 ms after you stop.
  - End of turn: Silero to detect speech, plus Smart Turn v3.2. Smart Turn
    listens to your intonation to tell a finished sentence from a thinking
    pause, so the assistant can reply after only 200 ms of silence.
  - The LLM: LFM2.5-1.2B, at about 125 tokens/s here and 820 MB. Qwen3.5-2B is
    the smarter option, at +180 ms and +600 MB.
